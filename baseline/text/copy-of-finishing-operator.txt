Quality Control CMM Programmer / Operator

 

Primary duties and responsibilities include:

 

Program set-up and operate CMM and Optical measuring machines.

Set-up CMM inspection for other operators / shifts.

Perform first piece, in process, and final inspection of machined parts.

Assist machine programmers and set-up personnel to optimize both milling and screw machine set-up time and machine utilization.

Train personnel to operate CMM and Optical measuring machines.

Completion of internal and customer required quality documentation.

 

Candidate shall meet the following requirements:

 

(3) years minimum CMM programming and operational experience (B & S / Hexagon PC DMIS preferred). Alternate CMM experience will be considered.

Optical Measuring device experience (OGP, Keyence, Nikon or similar).

Background in Quality/Mechanical Engineering a plus

Background in Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing,

SPC (MiniTab) experience a plus.

Capability to use all standard measuring tools such as optical comparators calipers, micrometers, go/no-go and thread gauges, surface plate instruments.

Able to use a computer and have a working knowledge of Microsoft Office.
(Outlook / Excel / Word / Power Point / Visio)

Ability to multi-task and be flexible in a fast-paced manufacturing environment.

Basic understanding of any of the following: ISO-13485:2016 , ISO 9001:2015 , TS16949, AS9100C,  21CFR820 quality systems.

 

Hours- 1st shift or 2nd  shift

Swing shift 10:00 am to 8:30 pm available.


We offer a competitive salary, medical and dental insurance, 401k, overtime, and advancement. EOE.
