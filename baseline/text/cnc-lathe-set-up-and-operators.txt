CNC Lathe Setup & Operator

This position is responsible for set-up and operating of HAAS 2 Axis Lathes and Omni Turn CNCs.

Responsible for the proper use of all measuring tools and devices used in inspections of all components. Works under general supervision.

​

Essential Job Functions include the following, but are not limited to:

Working knowledge of ISO quality Regulations & procedures

Responsible for set-up and operation of machines listed above in compliance with inch and metric drawing specifications

Possess the ability to set up and operate, lathes and other secondary equipment

Thorough understanding of blueprint reading

Works independently and inspects work to the specified standards on the drawings

General housekeeping in the work area which includes preventative maintenance on the machines

Perform other duties as required to ensure efficient operation of the shop

​

Experience:

Minimum 3 years of experience in a similar position is a plus

 

Education:

High School Diploma or equivalent or equivalent work experience

CNC Certification or CNC Trade School a major plus

CNC Lathe Programming knowledge is required

 

Specialized Knowledge and Skills:

Knowledge of medical products a plus

Strong sense of ownership and work ethic

Good communication skills

 

Job Type: Full-time

​Pay: $15.00 - $28.00 per hour
