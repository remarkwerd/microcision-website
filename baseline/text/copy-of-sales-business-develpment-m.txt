BOOKKEEPER

Microcision LLC, NJ (On-site)
$55,000 – $65,000/year
Full-time | Monday–Friday | First shift

​

About Microcision
Located in a thoroughly modern facility just outside downtown Philadelphia, in Pennsauken, NJ. Microcision is a leader in high precision medical device machining. Life-saving and life-enhancing medical devices are produced and designed every day in our facility.

​

Role Overview
We are seeking a detail-oriented and organized Bookkeeper to join our team. Reporting to the Vice President, the Bookkeeper will manage accounts payable/receivable, cash deposits, bank reconciliations, and client finances (for representative payee accounts). This role is essential for maintaining accurate financial records and supporting the overall financial health of the organization

​

Key Responsibilities

Process electronic accounts payable and receivable, ensuring timely payments and collections including maintaining a file of each account.

Perform account analysis to identify discrepancies and resolve issues promptly.

Prepare and post journal entries, including debits and credits, to the general ledger.

Assist with audits and financial documentation.

Maintain bank accounts and reconciliations.

Collaborate with other departments to ensure accurate financial reporting and compliance with Microcision policies.

Payroll back up.

​

Requirements

College degree in accounting is preferred

5+ years bookkeeping/accounting experience in a manufacturing environment

Strong written and verbal communication skills

Ability to work independently and use good judgment

Ability to commute to Pennsauken, NJ

​

Benefits

401(k), Health, dental, vision, life insurance, and Paid time off
