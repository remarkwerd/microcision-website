OUR CAPABILITIES

From design, prototype, to production, Microcision's capabilities include complex drilling, cross-drilling, turning, threading and milling of implants and components.   Every stage of manufacturing and quality is based on ISO 13485:2016 to assure complete traceability. Microcision has years of experience machining titanium, titanium alloys, all surgical grade stainless steels, cobalt chromium and medical grade plastics.
