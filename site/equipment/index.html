OUR EQUIPMENT

Microcision is located in Pennsauken, New Jersey, minutes from downtown Philadelphia and employs 85 people: operating two full shifts, six days per week.

With over 50 CNCs, Microcision utilizes a full range of advanced Swiss CNC, 5 -Axis Milling, EDM and many other types of advanced production equipment. Finishing, Assembly, Cleaning, Inspection and Packaging are all performed by staff members with extensive training in our custom-designed facility.
