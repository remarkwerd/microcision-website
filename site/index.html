SERVICES

Microcision is located in a thoroughly modern facility just outside downtown Philadelphia. Continuous investment targets the most sophisticated systems and machining technologies. Advanced CNC, Swiss Type, 5 Axis Milling Centers and wire EDM produce the most complicated miniature components as efficiently as possible. All products are inspected using vision systems, CMM, optical comparators, binocular microscopes, as well as digital inspection equipment. Product is delivered on time and as required including passivation, anodize, laser marking, complete assembly and packaging. 

CAPABILITIES

From design, product  development to full scale production, Microcision's capabilities include complex drilling, cross-drilling, turning, threading, and milling of implants and instruments.

Consistent, Accurate, On Time, and On Budget.

Microcision is a custom manufacturer of precision machined components, implants, and assemblies with tolerances as tight as +/- .0001. From product development through production, Microcision capabilities include complex turning, drilling, cross-drilling, threading and milling of small parts made from all medical grade metals and plastics. Our staff has extensive expertise in the design and manufacturing of medical products. Certified to ISO 13485:2016 standards, every process at Microcision ensures exact and consistent adherence to critical dimensions and lot traceability. Millions of dollars have been invested in state-of-the-art CNC technology, training, information and tracking systems. This allows Microcision to produce the most complex configuration in the most cost-effective manner.
